Account & Data Deletion

Unique Drawings, by Unique Properties · Last updated: 2026-07-09

Unique Drawings (package com.uniqueproperties.drawvault), powered by DrawVault, is a document management application operated by Unique Properties for authorized project teams. Accounts are created and managed by an administrator; the app has no public self-service sign-up. This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data.

How to request account and data deletion

  1. Email harshcollege06@gmail.com from an address associated with your account, or contact your DrawVault administrator directly.
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request — Unique Drawings" and include your full name and username so we can locate your account.
  3. We will verify that the request comes from the account holder or an authorized administrator.
  4. Once verified, we will deactivate your account and delete or de-identify eligible personal data, and confirm completion by email.

Deletion request contact: harshcollege06@gmail.com

What data is deleted

When a deletion request is verified and approved, we delete or de-identify the following account information where reasonably possible:

  • Your name, username, and avatar initials
  • Your password hash and authentication/session tokens
  • Your role assignment and project access settings
  • Your active/inactive account status record

What data may be retained, and for how long

Unique Drawings is a document-control system for construction projects. Certain project and audit records must be retained for legitimate business, legal, security, dispute-resolution, and construction-record purposes, even after an account is deleted. Where retained, records that reference a deleted account are de-identified where reasonably possible.
