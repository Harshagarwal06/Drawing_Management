Privacy Policy for Unique Drawings

Last updated: 2026-07-09

Unique Drawings, powered by DrawVault, is a drawing and document management application for project teams. The app is operated for Unique Properties and is intended for authorized project users only. Users need an account issued by the organization or project administrator to access the app.

This Privacy Policy explains how Unique Drawings collects, uses, shares, protects, retains, and deletes information when you use the Android app and related backend services.

Information We Collect

Unique Drawings collects the information needed to provide secure project document management.

Account and Profile Information

We collect account information created by an administrator, such as name, username, role, avatar initials, active or inactive status, password hash, and project access assignments.

Authentication and Security Information

We process login credentials, authentication tokens, token version data, request metadata, IP-derived rate limiting signals, security logs, upload limits, and access logs. On Android, session information is stored using secure device storage where available.

Project and Document Information

We collect project records, folders, drawings, drawing numbers, titles, revisions, disciplines, originators, statuses, uploaded files, document metadata, file paths, remarks, and related project activity.

Transmittal and Recipient Information

When users create transmittals, we collect recipient names and email addresses entered by the user, the selected drawings, generated transmittal PDFs, acknowledgments, comments, and delivery or acknowledgment status.

App Activity and Audit Information

We record app activity such as uploads, revisions, document views or downloads where logged by the service, transmittal creation, acknowledgments, void actions, deletes, folder changes, project changes, role changes, and other audit events needed for document control.

Files and Attachments

Users may upload drawings, PDFs, and related project documents. These files may contain personal information or project information depending on what users upload.

Optional Integrations

If enabled by an administrator, project notifications may be sent through email or Slack. Slack webhook configuration is stored only when configured by an authorized administrator.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Authenticate users and keep accounts secure.
  • Enforce role-based and project-based access control.
  • Store, organize, view, download, revise, and delete project drawings and documents.
  • Generate and send transmittals and acknowledgment records.
  • Maintain document control audit history.
  • Manage users, roles, projects, folders, and project settings.
  • Troubleshoot errors, reliability issues, and support requests.
  • Prevent unauthorized access, abuse, fraud, and excessive login or upload activity.
  • Comply with business, legal, project-record, and construction-record requirements.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information.

We share information only as needed to operate the app and project workflow:

  • Authorized project users can access project information according to their assigned role and project permissions.
  • Transmittal recipients receive the documents and information intentionally sent to them by an authorized user.
  • Administrators can manage users, roles, projects, settings, and project records.
  • Service providers may process information to provide hosting, storage, email delivery, Slack notification delivery, database services, security logging, and related infrastructure.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the organization, users, projects, or service.

Service Providers

Unique Drawings may use infrastructure and service providers such as:

  • Backend hosting providers.
  • Database and server infrastructure.
  • Cloudflare R2 or compatible object storage for private file storage.
  • Email delivery providers for transmittals.
  • Slack, if project notifications are configured by an administrator.
  • Google Play and Android platform services for app distribution and device operation.

These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to the app or organization.

Data Security

Unique Drawings uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption in transit in production.
  • Role-based access control.
  • Per-project authorization checks.
  • Password hashing.
  • JWT-based sessions with expiration.
  • Token revocation on logout, password changes, deactivation, and relevant account changes.
  • Login rate limiting and upload rate limiting.
  • Private object storage for uploaded files.
  • Short-lived authenticated signed URLs for file access.
  • Android secure storage for native session data where available.
  • Disabled Android app backup for sensitive app data.

No system can guarantee perfect security. Users should protect their credentials and report suspected unauthorized access to their administrator immediately.

Data Retention

Project records are retained for business, legal, construction-record, audit, dispute-resolution, and compliance purposes. This may include drawings, revisions, transmittals, acknowledgments, audit events, account records, and related metadata.

Retention periods may vary by project, contract, legal requirement, and organizational policy. The organization may retain certain records even after an account is deactivated or deleted if needed for legitimate business, legal, security, or project-record purposes.

Account and Data Deletion

Accounts are created and managed by authorized administrators. Users cannot create public self-service accounts in the app.

To request account deletion, account deactivation, access to personal information, correction of personal information, or deletion of eligible data, contact your DrawVault administrator or the privacy contact listed in this policy.

When an account deletion request is approved, the organization will delete or de-identify eligible account information where reasonably possible. Some records may be retained when required for project history, legal obligations, security, audit integrity, dispute resolution, or construction-record retention.

Children's Privacy

Unique Drawings is a business and project document management app. It is not directed to children and is not intended for use by children. Accounts should only be issued to authorized project users.

International Processing

Information may be processed and stored in countries where the organization or its service providers operate. By using the app, users understand that information may be transferred to and processed in those locations according to this policy and applicable law.

User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

  • Uploading only documents they are authorized to upload.
  • Sending transmittals only to intended recipients.
  • Keeping login credentials confidential.
  • Reporting incorrect access, suspected unauthorized access, or privacy concerns to an administrator.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When the policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes should be reviewed by the organization before publication and reflected in the app and Google Play Console.
